1. Citizen Kane · 《公民凯恩》

导演：Orson Welles（奥逊·威尔斯） 年份：1941 国家：USA 片长：119 分钟
English Summary

Often called the greatest film ever made, Citizen Kane follows the rise and fall of Charles Foster Kane, a fictional newspaper magnate modeled on William Randolph Hearst. Told through flashbacks and the investigations of a reporter trying to uncover the meaning of Kane's dying word, "Rosebud," the film broke cinematic ground with its deep-focus cinematography, low-angle shots, non-linear narrative, and overlapping dialogue. It topped the British Film Institute's Sight & Sound critics' poll for five consecutive decades (1962–2002).

中文翻译

《公民凯恩》常被誉为史上最伟大的电影。影片讲述了虚构报业大亨查尔斯·福斯特·凯恩（原型为威廉·伦道夫·赫斯特）的兴衰史。通过倒叙与一名记者追查凯恩临终遗言"玫瑰花蕾"的真相展开。影片以深焦摄影、低角度镜头、非线性叙事与重叠对白开创了电影语言的新纪元，并在 1962 至 2002 年间连续五届蝉联英国电影学会《视与听》影评人十年大评选的第一名。

推荐理由：电影叙事与摄影手法的"开山鼻祖"，每一位影迷都该至少看一次。

2. The Godfather · 《教父》

导演：Francis Ford Coppola（弗朗西斯·福特·科波拉） 年份：1972 国家：USA 片长：175 分钟
English Summary

The Godfather chronicles the Corleone mafia family under patriarch Vito Corleone and the gradual transformation of his youngest son, Michael, from a reluctant outsider into a ruthless boss. Adapted from Mario Puzo's bestselling novel, the film is praised for Marlon Brando and Al Pacino's performances, Gordon Willis's shadowy cinematography, Nino Rota's score, and its portrayal of family, loyalty, and power. It won the Academy Award for Best Picture in 1972 and is regularly named the greatest American film by the American Film Institute.

中文翻译

《教父》讲述了科里昂黑手党家族在教父维托·科里昂的领导下，以及他最年轻的儿子迈克尔从一名不愿介入家族事务的外人，逐步蜕变为冷血大佬的故事。影片改编自马里奥·普佐的畅销小说。马龙·白兰度与阿尔·帕西诺的表演、戈登·威利斯的暗调摄影、尼诺·罗塔的配乐以及对家族、忠诚与权力的刻画都备受赞誉。本片斩获 1972 年奥斯卡最佳影片，并常年位列美国电影学会评出的"美国最伟大电影"榜首。

推荐理由：黑帮电影的巅峰，也是关于家族与人性最深沉的史诗。

3. Tokyo Story · 《东京物语》

导演：Yasujirō Ozu（小津安二郎） 年份：1953 国家：Japan 片长：136 分钟
English Summary

An aging couple travel from their small hometown of Onomichi to Tokyo to visit their grown children, only to find that their sons and daughters are too busy to spend time with them. Their widowed daughter-in-law Noriko is the only one who truly makes them feel welcome. Ozu's restrained, contemplative style — low camera placed at tatami-mat height, sparse cuts, and ellipses — turns an ordinary family visit into a meditation on generational change, postwar Japan's Westernization, and the inevitable distance between parents and children. It was voted the greatest film of all time in the 2012 Sight & Sound directors' poll.

中文翻译

一对老夫妇从广岛县的小镇尾道前往东京探望已成年的儿女，却发现子女们各自忙碌无暇陪伴，唯有丧夫的儿媳妇纪子对他们悉心照料。小津安二郎克制、内敛的影像风格——低机位（模拟榻榻米视角）、稀疏的剪辑与留白——把一次普通的探亲之旅升华为对代际变迁、日本战后西化以及亲子间渐行渐远的沉思。该片在 2012 年《视与听》导演评选中被评为影史第一。

推荐理由：东方式含蓄美学的极致，看完后会让你想给家里打个电话。

4. Vertigo · 《迷魂记》

导演：Alfred Hitchcock（阿尔弗雷德·希区柯克） 年份：1958 国家：USA 片长：128 分钟
English Summary

Retired San Francisco detective John "Scottie" Ferguson, suffering from acrophobia after a rooftop incident, is hired to follow the wife of an old acquaintance. What begins as a surveillance job becomes an obsessive, doomed romance that plays on themes of memory, desire, and control. Hitchcock pioneered the famous "dolly zoom" (now called the Vertigo effect) to convey Scottie's disorientation on heights. Initially a commercial disappointment, the film was re-evaluated and eventually crowned the greatest film ever made in the 2012 Sight & Sound critics' poll.

中文翻译

退休的旧金山警探约翰·"斯科蒂"·弗格森在一次屋顶追捕后患上了恐高症，受雇跟踪一位旧识的妻子。原本只是一桩监视任务，却演变成一段执念深重、无可挽回的恋情，触及记忆、欲望与掌控的主题。希区柯克首创了著名的"滑动变焦"（如今被称为"迷魂记效果"）以表现斯科蒂的眩晕。影片上映之初票房失利，但被重新评价后最终于 2012 年《视与听》影评人评选中登顶影史第一。

推荐理由：希区柯克最私人化的杰作，关于"为什么我们会爱上不该爱的人"。

5. 2001: A Space Odyssey · 《2001 太空漫游》

导演：Stanley Kubrick（斯坦利·库布里克） 年份：1968 国家：UK / USA 片长：139 分钟
English Summary

Kubrick's epic traces humanity's evolution from a prehistoric tribe that encounters a mysterious monolith to a future space voyage to Jupiter, where astronaut Dave Bowman confronts another — and is transformed into a "Star Child." With dialogue kept to a minimum and long stretches accompanied only by classical music (Strauss, Ligeti, Khachaturian), the film explores human evolution, artificial intelligence (the calmly menacing HAL 9000), and the possibility of extraterrestrial intelligence. It topped the 2022 Sight & Sound directors' poll.

中文翻译

库布里克的史诗巨作追溯了人类从一支遭遇神秘黑石碑的原始部落，到未来飞向木星的太空旅程——宇航员戴夫·鲍曼在那里遭遇了第三块黑石碑，并被转化为"星孩"。影片对白极少，长段落仅以古典音乐（施特劳斯、利盖蒂、哈恰图良）相伴，探讨人类进化、人工智能（冷静而危险的 HAL 9000）以及地外文明的可能性。该片登顶 2022 年《视与听》导演评选第一名。

推荐理由：科幻片的源头，也是关于人类文明最冷静也最震撼的寓言。

6. Jeanne Dielman, 23, quai du Commerce, 1080 Bruxelles · 《让娜·迪尔曼》

导演：Chantal Akerman（香特尔·阿克曼） 年份：1975 国家：Belgium / France 片长：201 分钟
English Summary

Over three days, Chantal Akerman's groundbreaking film observes the meticulously ordered routine of Jeanne Dielman, a widowed Brussels housewife who supports herself by receiving gentlemen in the afternoon. Shot in long takes with a static camera and no close-ups, the film turns cooking, cleaning, and small domestic rituals into the substance of cinema. Small disruptions in Jeanne's routine — overcooked potatoes, a dropped brush — slowly accumulate into an act of devastating violence. In 2022, it became the first film directed by a woman to top the Sight & Sound critics' poll.

中文翻译

香特尔·阿克曼的这部开创性作品用三天时间，观察布鲁塞尔寡妇让娜·迪尔曼按部就班的日常生活——她靠下午接待男客维持生计。影片以长镜头、静止机位、无特写拍摄，把做饭、打扫和琐碎的家居仪式本身变成了电影语言。日常生活里细微的裂痕——煮过头的土豆、掉落的刷子——一点点累积，最终爆发为一次毁灭性的暴力。在 2022 年，让娜·迪尔曼成为《视与听》影评人评选史上首部由女性导演夺得第一名的电影。

推荐理由：慢电影与女性主义电影的里程碑，需要耐心，但回报巨大。
